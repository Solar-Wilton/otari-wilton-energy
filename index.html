Could Otari-Wilton become a community energy pilot?

We’re exploring solar, battery resilience, neighbourhood energy savings and possible small-scale wind feasibility for community sites and nearby homes around Otari-Wilton.

Register interest Learn more
Solar + batteries + local resilience A practical pilot idea for community buildings, neighbours and emergency backup power.

The idea

This is not a fixed proposal yet. The first step is simply to find out whether local residents, community organisations, Council and energy partners are interested in exploring a practical community energy project.

🏠

Neighbourhood bulk-buy

Interested homes could explore group pricing for solar, battery-ready systems or battery storage.

🔋

Community resilience

Suitable community sites could investigate solar and backup battery power for outages or civil defence use.

🌬️

Wind feasibility

Because Wilton is hilly and windy, small-scale wind could be investigated as an add-on if it stacks up.

Why Otari-Wilton?

The area has a rare mix of community facilities, nearby homes, bush reserve identity and a strong case for resilience planning.

✓
Community anchor sites nearby
Potential links with the community centre, preschool, lawn bowls club and other local organisations.
✓
Resilience value
Local battery storage could help community facilities stay useful during outages or emergency events.
✓
Replicable pilot
If this works here, the model could be shared with other Wellington suburbs.

What happens next?

The project will only move forward if there is enough community interest and the technical, legal and financial details make sense.

Gather local interest

Collect names from residents, community groups and potential anchor sites.

Speak to key partners

Approach Council, community organisations, Wellington Electricity and solar/battery installers.

Request early feasibility advice

Check roof suitability, likely system sizes, battery options, network limits and whether wind is worth exploring.

Share the findings

Report back to interested locals before any decisions or commitments are made.
